Website analytics & privacy

We use Google Analytics to measure visits, referral sources and clicks on download buttons. Cookies distinguish browsers; visitor counts are estimates, not named people. Advertising signals and ad personalization are disabled.

Only public page paths, referral origins and supported campaign tags are explicitly sent. We do not send arbitrary URL queries, chat content or app activity. Google processes analytics requests and browser/device information under its own privacy policy. Website analytics is separate from the local Mote Mac app.

The exclusion choice is saved in this browser. It applies to both languages, persists across visits, and prevents the Google tag from loading. Clearing site data, using private browsing or switching browsers requires opting out again. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track are also respected.

Checking preference…

Download clicks are not completed downloads or installations. Ad blockers and opt-outs can reduce counts.
